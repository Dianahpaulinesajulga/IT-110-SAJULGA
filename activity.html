HTML DOM

Steps to make my DOM screenshot:

  1. Open this page in the browser.
  2. Right-click on the page.
  3. Choose Inspect.
  4. Open the Elements panel.
  5. Find the elements with IDs.
  6. Show at least 5 nested elements.
  7. Take a screenshot.
  8. Save it as dom-tree.png.
